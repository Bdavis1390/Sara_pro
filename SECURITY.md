# Worldshepherd / SARA Security Boundary

## Repository classification

This GitHub repository is **PUBLIC-ONLY**. It is an engineering and evidence repository, not an authorized Federal Contract Information (FCI), Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), export-controlled technical-data, classified-information, or operational mission-data environment.

Do **not** commit or upload:

- FCI that is not intended for public release;
- CUI, Covered Defense Information, or controlled technical information;
- classified information at any level;
- ITAR- or EAR-controlled technical data unless an authorized export-control process has explicitly approved the repository and recipients;
- government-furnished sensitive information;
- real operational mission data, credentials, tokens, private keys, passwords, certificates containing private key material, or recovery secrets;
- sensitive personal, personnel, financial, or medical information.

Synthetic, sanitized, public, or explicitly releasable engineering data may be used for demonstrations when provenance is retained.

## Compliance status

Repository controls, CI evidence, security scans, audit records, and system-boundary artifacts are **compliance-readiness evidence only**. They do not by themselves establish:

- CMMC status or certification;
- a NIST SP 800-171 assessment score;
- an SPRS score;
- an Authority to Operate (ATO) or RMF authorization;
- a Facility Clearance (FCL) or personnel clearance;
- SAM, UEI, CAGE, JCP/DD Form 2345, ITAR, EAR, or other registration/certification status;
- authorization to process FCI or CUI.

Any proposal or contract requiring those statuses must pass the applicable evidence gate before Worldshepherd represents compliance.

## CMMC / NIST basis

Worldshepherd may use NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 3 as a forward engineering baseline. For CMMC Level 2, the applicable CMMC requirement set remains the 110 security requirements aligned to NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 unless the governing regulation or solicitation changes. Contract-specific clauses control.

## Vulnerability reporting

Do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities, credentials, or sensitive evidence in a public GitHub issue. Use a private repository security-reporting channel when available, or an authorized private channel to the repository owner.

## External-action boundary

Security and compliance claims require identified-human authorization and objective evidence. Automated workflows may collect evidence and fail closed; they may not certify compliance, submit government representations, sign attestations, or make binding statements on behalf of the organization.
