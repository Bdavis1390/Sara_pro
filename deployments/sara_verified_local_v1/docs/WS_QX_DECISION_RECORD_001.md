# ADR WS-QX-001 — Qualification evidence must be first-class

Decision: Worldshepherd will treat evidence custody and claim promotion as first-class architecture rather than proposal/documentation conventions.

Context: recurring opportunities require combinations of software assurance, physical performance, partner evidence, external validation, and program-specific eligibility. Averaging these into one readiness score or treating a requirement as evidence creates unacceptable claim ambiguity.

Decision consequences:

- qualification profiles declare independent mandatory dimensions;
- evidence vectors preserve physical, assurance/TEVV, and replication/external axes;
- claim promotion is fail-closed;
- physical and external claims require separate interlocks;
- PRE forecasts can create experiments but cannot upgrade maturity;
- partner evidence retains originating provenance;
- solicitation crosswalks remain requirements mappings, not compliance claims;
- negative and inconclusive results remain part of the evidence graph.

Status: ACCEPTED for WS-QX 0.1 release candidate.
