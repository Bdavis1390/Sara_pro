# WS-QX Negative Evidence Policy

Failed, null, contradictory, and inconclusive experiments are evidence and must not disappear from the qualification record merely because they do not support promotion.

A failed run should retain its manifest, configuration, measurements, faults, uncertainty, failed dimensions, and evidence digest. PRE should use repeated negative evidence to revise forecasts, experiment design, partner selection, and capability-development priorities.

A later successful run does not erase an earlier failure. The evidence graph should explain why results differ: configuration change, corrected defect, altered environment, improved hardware/model, calibration correction, or unresolved contradiction.

This policy is essential for scientific integrity and for preventing survivorship bias in opportunity capture.
