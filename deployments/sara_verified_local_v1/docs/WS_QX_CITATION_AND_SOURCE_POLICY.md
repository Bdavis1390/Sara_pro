# WS-QX Source and Citation Policy

Source material can establish a requirement, prior-art statement, literature support, standard definition, partner assertion, or experimental result reported by another party. It does not establish that Worldshepherd reproduced that result.

Every source-derived Requirement Delta should preserve enough provenance to recover the authoritative source, date/version, relevant statement, and evidence class. Conflicting sources should remain visible rather than being silently reconciled.

Official solicitation/program sources control opportunity facts where available. Peer-reviewed literature, government laboratories, standards bodies, universities, and manufacturer documentation should be selected according to the claim being supported. Secondary reporting may aid discovery but should not silently replace the controlling source for consequential requirements.
