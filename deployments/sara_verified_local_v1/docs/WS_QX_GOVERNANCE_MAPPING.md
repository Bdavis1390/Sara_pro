# WS-QX Governance Mapping

## PRE
Inputs authoritative requirements, recurrence, forecasts, standards, partner needs, and observed gaps. Outputs Requirement Delta Records and proposed qualification work. PRE cannot promote evidence or maturity.

## SARA
Executes bounded qualification workflows from declared manifests. Captures workflow identity, configuration, result, failure, and recovery evidence.

## ECHO SENTINEL LINK
Maintains evidence lineage, source identity, transformations, digests, timestamps, and links among raw, normalized, derived, partner, and external evidence.

## PRIME SENTINEL
Evaluates whether evidence prerequisites authorize a claim-state transition. Defaults to denial when mandatory evidence is absent, contradictory, stale, outside scope, or lacks required provenance.

## OVERWATCH
Displays evidence state, qualification dimensions, unresolved deltas, contradictions, expirations, and readiness without converting visibility into approval.

## Human authority
High-consequence execution, external release, physical tests requiring human safety controls, partner representations, and consequential claim publication remain subject to human authorization under the applicable Worldshepherd workflow.
