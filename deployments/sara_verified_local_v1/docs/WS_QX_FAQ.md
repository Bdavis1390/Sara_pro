# WS-QX 0.1 FAQ

**Does a green CI run mean a capability is physically validated?** No. It supports only the software/test claims actually exercised.

**Does matching a solicitation threshold mean Worldshepherd is eligible?** No. Program eligibility, prior-evidence requirements, timing, entity status, and proposal rules are separate.

**Does SHA-256 prove a measurement is true?** No. It provides an integrity binding for recorded content; measurement validity requires provenance, calibration, controls, and uncertainty evidence.

**Can a partner's claim be copied into Worldshepherd's claim state?** No. Partner evidence is ingested with origin and scope; Worldshepherd evaluates what it supports.

**Can a high average readiness score compensate for one missing mandatory dimension?** Not under a fail-closed profile.

**Are failed experiments deleted once a later run passes?** No. Negative and contradictory evidence remain part of the evidence graph.

**Is WS-SEMCAP-01 a DARPA compliance certification?** No. It is a reusable Worldshepherd qualification profile informed by the semantic-edge problem class.
