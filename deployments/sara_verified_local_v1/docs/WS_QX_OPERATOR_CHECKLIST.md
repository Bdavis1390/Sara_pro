# WS-QX Operator Checklist

Before a qualification run:

- identify qualification profile/version and exact manifest;
- bind software/model/configuration versions;
- identify hardware and calibration where applicable;
- verify authorization and safety controls;
- verify raw evidence retention and clock/provenance sources;
- declare baselines and thresholds before observing results;
- record expected fault dispositions;
- preserve stop/abort criteria.

During the run:

- retain raw evidence before normalization where practical;
- record faults, overrides, interruptions, restarts, and anomalies;
- do not edit thresholds to turn an observed failure into a pass;
- abort when a mandatory safety interlock fails.

After the run:

- finalize evidence digest;
- evaluate dimensions independently;
- preserve failures and inconclusive results;
- promote only the claim state permitted by observed evidence;
- leave physical/external/program claims false unless their separate prerequisites are met;
- create a Requirement Delta Record for every unresolved mandatory gap.
