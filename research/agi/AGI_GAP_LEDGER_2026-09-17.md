## Remaining blockers before Worldshepherd may use COMPETENT_AGI

1. Independent human-referenced breadth across most preregistered cognitive families.
2. Independent long-horizon evidence across more than software/ML/cyber.
3. Novel skill acquisition outside ARC-style environments.
4. Persistent state continuity under compaction and reset.
5. Skill abstraction and structural transfer.
6. Rule-change adaptation without catastrophic forgetting.
7. Metacognitive calibration and identifiability awareness.
8. Causal intervention prediction.
9. Real-world tool/computer reliability under distribution shift.
10. External blind replication of the complete candidate system.
