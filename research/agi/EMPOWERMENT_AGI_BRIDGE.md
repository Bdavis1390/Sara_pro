# Empowerment Bridge: From Internal Dynamics to General Agency

## 1. Why this is the missing quantitative concept

The user's original lesson concerns the ability of an internally changing conscious organism to produce changes in the surrounding world.

Energy conservation alone does not quantify this ability.

Information theory does.

**Empowerment** measures how much causal control an agent's available actions give it over its future states or observations.

For an initial state s and action sequence A over horizon T:

Empowerment(s) = max I(A ; S_future | S_initial=s)

where the maximization is over possible action distributions.

The exact implementation varies with model assumptions, but the central meaning is stable:

**How many reliably distinguishable futures can this agent intentionally select among?**

## 2. Connection to Agency Amplification

A neural control signal can be energetically tiny while selecting among large external energy flows.

Examples:
- motor neuron -> muscle metabolism;
- finger motion -> electrical switch -> machine;
- BCI signal -> robotic arm battery;
- speech -> group coordination -> infrastructure.

The originating signal does not create the downstream energy.

It selects or gates a causal pathway.

Thus two quantities should be separated:

- energetic magnitude;
- causal control capacity.

The latter is closer to empowerment.

## 3. Internal frequencies fit into this framework

A frequency or oscillatory pattern matters when it changes:
- sensing;
- prediction;
- action selection;
- actuator timing;
- synchronization;
- control bandwidth;
- feedback stability.

Therefore the scientifically useful question is not:

"Does frequency X change the universe?"

It is:

"Does controlled change in internal dynamical variable X increase, decrease, or redirect the agent's causal control over future world states through a specified pathway?"

That can be experimentally measured.

## 4. Thermodynamics + empowerment

The new non-equilibrium consciousness result suggests conscious states are associated with richer non-equilibrium organization.

Empowerment asks what those organized states can **do** through action.

This suggests a new research hypothesis:

> Organized non-equilibrium dynamics may support a larger or more adaptive repertoire of controllable sensorimotor futures than less organized states.

This is not established.

It is testable.

For each brain/agent state, measure:
- non-equilibrium/FDT violation;
- complexity/criticality;
- action repertoire;
- action-outcome mutual information;
- empowerment;
- metabolic cost;
- robustness under perturbation.

Then ask whether organization predicts control capacity after controlling for raw energy expenditure.

## 5. AGI relevance

General intelligence and consciousness must remain distinct hypotheses.

An AGI could in principle be highly capable without being conscious.

However, increasingly general **agency** requires more than static language prediction.

A robust general agent needs to maintain a loop:

world -> observation -> internal model -> uncertainty -> policy -> action -> changed world -> new observation.

That loop is exactly where the original lesson becomes useful.

The system must preserve:
- relationships rather than only surface tokens;
- causal dependencies rather than only correlations;
- state transitions rather than isolated snapshots;
- representation losses;
- provenance;
- uncertainty;
- action consequences;
- feedback.

## 6. Worldshepherd AGI stepping-stone

Worldshepherd already contains pieces that map naturally onto this architecture:

### Relational Evidence Envelope
Prevents flattening multimodal evidence into one representation.

### Representation-Loss Budget
Records what disappears during summarization/translation/transformation.

### EBOM
Tracks what every claim depends on.

### EIX
Determines which downstream claims are actually affected when evidence changes.

### SARA
Builds world/evidence models and proposes action.

### PRIME
Bounds authorized action.

### ECHO
Retains evidence/provenance and change history.

### OVERWATCH
Tracks system state, uncertainty and dependency blast radius.

These are not proof of AGI.

But they are components of a safer general-agent architecture because they force the agent to represent:

**what it knows, how it knows it, what it can change, what it is allowed to change, and what would falsify its current model.**

## 7. Four empowerment states

Worldshepherd should distinguish:

### Potential empowerment
Maximum theoretical influence over future states.

### Demonstrated empowerment
Control actually measured in experiments.

### Authorized empowerment
Subset allowed by governance.

### Safe empowerment
Authorized subset that remains within validated operational envelopes.

This prevents a category error:

more agency != better system.

For high-consequence systems, the target is:

high competence + calibrated epistemics + bounded empowerment.

## 8. A stronger AGI criterion

A useful candidate benchmark for general agency is not merely broad question-answering.

It is whether an agent can repeatedly:

1. infer a latent world state from incomplete multimodal evidence;
2. preserve uncertainty and provenance;
3. identify information lost in its representations;
4. choose information-gathering actions;
5. construct causal hypotheses;
6. predict intervention outcomes;
7. act within authorization bounds;
8. observe consequences;
9. revise its world model;
10. know when its evidence no longer supports its previous claim.

This is the same layered systems lesson extended from a symbolic image to an acting epistemic system.
