# Embodied Consciousness Model

## What the user's argument establishes well

The exclusion argument is important:

- loss of vision does not abolish consciousness;
- loss of hearing does not abolish consciousness;
- loss of touch or motor function does not necessarily abolish consciousness.

Therefore consciousness should not be modeled as equivalent to any single sensory or motor faculty.

Sensory systems are best modeled as **channels that contribute content**.
Motor systems are best modeled as **channels that permit action/report**.

Neither is, by itself, a definition of consciousness.

## What neuroscience supports

Human conscious states depend on functioning brain activity.

Brain function depends on:
- ATP-supported metabolism;
- maintenance of membrane potentials and ion gradients;
- neurotransmission;
- coordinated activity across neuronal networks;
- interactions with glia, vasculature and the rest of the body.

EEG measures voltage fluctuations generated by coordinated neural electrical activity.
It is a measurement of brain dynamics, not a direct measurement of a distinct "consciousness energy."

Likewise:
- fMRI measures blood-oxygenation-related signals;
- PET can measure metabolism or molecular processes;
- MEG measures magnetic fields generated by neural currents.

These modalities provide evidence about neural conditions associated with conscious state.

## Why "consciousness = energy" is too strong

Energy is indispensable to neural activity, but amount of energy alone does not uniquely determine consciousness.

Examples:
- unconscious states can retain substantial cerebral metabolism;
- sleep and anesthesia do not reduce brain energy use to zero;
- some disorders of consciousness preserve measurable brain activity;
- altered network organization/connectivity can matter more than raw energetic magnitude.

Therefore the stronger scientific model is:

**energy enables the process; organized dynamics instantiate the state.**

## Working systems model

Let:

- M(t) = metabolic energy flux;
- V(t) = electrochemical state variables;
- X(t) = neural-network organization/dynamics;
- B(t) = bodily/interoceptive state;
- S_i(t) = sensory input channels;
- A(t) = motor/output channels;
- R(t) = overt report;
- C(t) = latent conscious-state variable.

A conservative model is:

dX/dt = F(X, V, M, B, S_1...S_n) + noise

R(t) = G(X, A, context)

C(t) is inferred from organized neural dynamics and behavior/physiology rather than equated with any single measured signal.

This preserves the user's central intuition while avoiding an unsupported identity claim.

## Death distinction

Brain death is defined clinically as complete and permanent cessation of brain function.

A body can retain local cellular metabolism or be mechanically supported after brain death, so "the body still contains energy" is not sufficient for consciousness.

The key distinction is not:

energy present vs energy absent

but:

organized, living brain function capable of supporting conscious state vs permanent cessation of brain function.

## Worldshepherd claim ladder

### Supported
- consciousness is not reducible to sight/hearing/touch;
- overt report is not identical to consciousness;
- neural function requires metabolic/electrochemical energy;
- conscious states correlate with specific organized neural dynamics;
- brain death is permanent cessation of brain function.

### Hypothesis
- consciousness is best modeled as an embodied, organized dynamical process of brain-body activity.

### Not currently claimed
- consciousness is a separate energy substance;
- EEG directly measures consciousness itself;
- a single frequency uniquely defines consciousness;
- consciousness has been scientifically demonstrated to exist independently of living brain function.

## Extension of the original lesson

The same non-flattening rule applies here:

brain energy != brain activity
brain activity != network organization
network organization != sensory content
sensory content != consciousness
motor report != consciousness
consciousness != any one measurement

The analysis target is the **relationship among all of them**.
