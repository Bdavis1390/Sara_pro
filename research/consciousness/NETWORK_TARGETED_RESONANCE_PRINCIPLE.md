# Network-Targeted Resonance Principle

## 1. Frequency alone is not the causal object

The consciousness research path now converges on a stronger rule:

**A rhythm matters because of where it originates, where it arrives, how it is encoded, which network state it enters, and what causal pathway it recruits.**

This is the physiological counterpart of the original layered-systems lesson.

A bare number such as 10 Hz is analogous to an isolated glyph:
it lacks the relational context required to infer function.

## 2. Cross-frequency conscious-state channel

Toker et al. (eLife 2024) identify a reproducible motif across species:

low-frequency signal (~1-13 Hz)
-> cross-region transfer
-> high-gamma encoding (52-104 Hz)

between cortex and thalamus.

The motif:
- is present during waking conscious states;
- is reduced during propofol loss of consciousness;
- is reduced during generalized spike-wave seizures;
- is enhanced under 5-MeO-DMT in the tested animals.

Their modeling links transfer strength to proximity of low-frequency thalamocortical dynamics to edge-of-chaos criticality.

This supports:

frequency relationship + region relationship + dynamical regime

rather than:

single frequency.

## 3. DBS demonstrates topology dependence

Warren et al. (Nature Communications 2025) analyzed 40 patients with chronic disorders of consciousness undergoing thalamic DBS.

Eleven patients showed categorical improvement by 12 months.

Outcome associations involved:
- preserved gray matter/striatal integrity;
- a stimulation sweet spot inferior to the centromedian nucleus;
- engagement of the inferior parafascicular/subparafascicular region;
- structural pathways resembling portions of the ventral tegmental tract;
- a functional network overlapping independent consciousness-impairment datasets.

Crucially, programmed stimulation amplitude, frequency and pulse width did not differ between improved and non-improved groups.

This does not prove frequency is irrelevant.
It shows that **frequency cannot be interpreted independently of where the energy is deposited and which network it engages.**

Limitations:
- uncontrolled cohort;
- only a subset improved;
- some localization statistics were uncorrected and did not survive FDR;
- association does not equal proof of a uniquely causal target.

## 4. Adversarial AI adds causal discovery

Toker et al. (Nature Neuroscience 2026) use:
- a deep consciousness detector;
- interpretable neural-field models;
- adversarial optimization between them.

The framework reproduced conscious/comatose dynamics across species and generated mechanistic predictions subsequently supported with:
- DTI in DOC patients;
- human coma tissue RNA sequencing;
- rat stroke tissue;
- human electrophysiology.

This is important methodologically.

The system did not stop at classification.

It moved:

detect
-> simulate
-> generate mechanism
-> predict intervention
-> test with independent modality.

That is the same reasoning architecture Worldshepherd has been developing across evidence domains.

## 5. State-space model

Represent a consciousness perturbation as:

P = {
  source_region,
  target_region,
  frequency_band,
  amplitude,
  phase,
  waveform,
  spatial_field,
  connectivity,
  tissue_integrity,
  criticality,
  non_equilibrium_state,
  directionality,
  feedback
}

Outcome O is modeled as:

O = F(P, baseline_state, subject_context)

not:

O = F(frequency).

## 6. Network resonance

Use "resonance" narrowly and operationally.

A candidate network-resonant effect should require:
- known source/target;
- measurable transfer;
- phase/frequency relationship;
- perturbation response;
- dose response;
- matched sham/null;
- reproducibility.

Numerical frequency coincidence without these is not resonance evidence.

## 7. Experimental synthesis

### A. Cross-frequency transfer
Measure:
- cortex-to-thalamus transfer entropy;
- thalamus-to-cortex transfer entropy;
- low-to-high frequency mapping;
- consciousness state.

### B. Targeted perturbation
Manipulate:
- same frequency at different targets;
- different frequencies at same target;
- same target under different baseline criticality states.

This directly separates:
frequency effect
from network-location effect
from state-dependence.

### C. Topology ablation
Compare models:
H0 frequency only
H1 frequency + amplitude
H2 frequency + spatial target
H3 frequency + target + connectivity
H4 full dynamical/topological state

Primary question:
How much predictive information is gained by adding relational structure?

## 8. Relation to organized-energy hypothesis

The strongest current formulation is now:

**Consciousness may depend on organized, network-specific, far-from-equilibrium energy and information flow whose function is defined by relational topology and state.**

That is more specific and more falsifiable than "consciousness is energy" or "consciousness is a frequency."
