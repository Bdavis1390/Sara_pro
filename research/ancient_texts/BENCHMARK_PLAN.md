# ATIP benchmark expansion plan

## Purpose

Prevent corpus scale from reducing epistemic discipline.

## Required benchmark quartet per release

1. Known-deciphered positive control.
2. Partially constrained/calibration corpus.
3. Undeciphered negative control.
4. Synthetic null.

## Initial benchmark families

- Linear B: reproduce structural constraints and held-out readings where contamination allows.
- Egyptian or cuneiform: second known-script control.
- Enochian: manuscript/editorial-drift calibration and session-blind holdout.
- Linear A and/or Indus: structure-only negative control.
- Synthetic controls: shuffled, first-order Markov, layout-preserving and adversarially planted patterns.

## Metrics vector

- inventory recovery
- segmentation agreement
- morphology hypotheses
- semantic-role alignment
- unsupported insertions
- untranslated residue
- positional regularity
- null-model exceedance
- contamination class
- confidence calibration
- claim ceiling

## Publication rule

A surprising output is not a discovery until:
1. source witness is reproducible,
2. comparator was appropriately withheld,
3. nulls and look-elsewhere effects are handled,
4. alternate explanations are documented,
5. independent/specialist review is complete.
