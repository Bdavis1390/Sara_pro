# ATIP v0.1 Publication Notes

## Public milestone

ATIP v0.1 publishes the first integrated Worldshepherd substrate for:

- provenance-preserving symbolic and ancient-text records;
- topology-aware comparison;
- Enochian historical calibration;
- decipherment contamination accounting;
- undeciphered-script fail-closed translation controls;
- source rights and federation policy;
- deterministic null-model diagnostics;
- typed evidence graphs;
- translation lineage auditing;
- pre-registered cross-corpus benchmark design.

## Important limitation

v0.1 is a **methods/software publication**, not a claim of new ancient-script decipherment.

The next evidence milestone is execution of the pre-registered benchmark cycle and public reporting of both positive and negative results.

## Verification status

Repository CI must pass before promotion to main.
