## Program channels — not substitutes for an L3 lab

### NSF Project Triad

Project Triad is designed to integrate quantum sensing, networking, and computing, with NQVL, X-Labs, and Quantum+X components. It is strategically aligned with heterogeneous quantum interconnects and system integration.

Use as:
- transition ecosystem;
- future demonstration venue;
- industry/academia/government integration path.

Do not count program alignment as device validation.

### NSF EPMQD

The Electronic, Photonic, Magnetic, and Quantum Devices program currently accepts full proposals anytime and explicitly includes heterogeneous integration, cryogenic/low-noise control and readout, scalable interconnects, reliability, validation, and quantum networking/computing devices.

Use as:
- academic-partner proposal channel;
- validation-methodology funding route;
- heterogeneous-integration support.

Do not treat proposal eligibility or submission as experimental evidence.

Every L3 inquiry should include:

- one-page WS-QPHONON technical hypothesis;
- claims boundary: architecture integrated, physical capability not claimed;
- L3 partner-experiment sequence;
- exact Worldshepherd software/governance contribution;
- requested partner hardware role;
- precommitted holdout/reproducibility requirement;
- data/provenance handling proposal;
- statement that negative results will be retained and reported.

## Promotion rule

A positive reply, meeting, NDA, research discussion, proposal, or planned experiment does not promote WS-QPHONON.

L3 promotion still requires relevant external phononic quantum hardware, a Worldshepherd-governed experiment, reproducible physical evidence, successful holdout prediction, uncertainty bounds, full ECHO provenance, and independent partner/human reproduction.
