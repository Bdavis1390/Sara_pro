# Worldshepherd Evidence-Governed Physical Engineering OS v0.1

Status: DRAFT BASELINE
Authority namespace: CRE1AWS
Administrative operator namespace: SSPADAWANZZ

## 1. Category

Worldshepherd is an **Evidence-Governed Physical Engineering Operating System**.

Its purpose is to shorten the path from scientific hypothesis to manufacturable, physically validated autonomous system while preserving the evidence chain needed to explain why every promoted claim is believed.

## 2. Core closed loop

SOURCE -> HYPOTHESIS -> MODEL -> SIMULATION -> MATERIAL/PROCESS -> BUILD -> CALIBRATION -> EXPERIMENT -> RAW EVIDENCE -> UNCERTAINTY -> RESULT -> CLAIM -> REPLICATION -> MODEL UPDATE

Every stage must retain provenance to the preceding stage. No simulation, literature statement, or hypothesis may be silently promoted to measured evidence.

## 3. Evidence classes

Canonical evidence classes:

- MEASURED: directly measured physical observation.
- CALCULATED: derived from measured values with a declared method.
- LITERATURE: sourced from external publication/data/reference.
- SIMULATED: generated by a model or solver.
- HYPOTHESIS: proposed explanation or unverified mechanism.
- NULL_CONTROL: known or designed negative/control condition.
- ARTIFACT: identified measurement or apparatus artifact.

Every result and claim SHALL carry one or more evidence classes plus provenance identifiers.

## 4. Worldshepherd-owned moat

Worldshepherd should own and improve:

1. Engineering evidence ontology.
2. Claims-control and promotion gates.
3. Experiment orchestration and provenance.
4. Solver credibility and experiment-correlation layer.
5. Materials/process/property graph.
6. ITX/UPB experimental methodology.
7. FIELD-SKIN system architecture and physical-state interface.
8. Propulsion allocation and physical-capability interface.
9. SARA governance, audit, and evidence graph.
10. Deterministic exploration/Glob methodology when it outperforms baseline DOE methods.
11. Cross-domain physical digital thread.

Worldshepherd should initially integrate rather than recreate generic CAD, CFD/FEA/EM solvers, manufacturing machines, and mission-autonomy products when qualified external systems provide better capability.

## 5. Common physical state

A common physical-system state SHOULD support at least:

X = {E, H, B, J, rho_e, rho, u, p, T, stress, material_state, V, I, position, velocity, angular_rate}

Subsystems MAY extend this state but SHALL NOT redefine common quantities incompatibly.

## 6. Common propulsion interface

Every propulsion module SHALL expose a common outer contract:

PROP = {force_vector, torque_vector, input_power, useful_power, mass_flow, thermal_load, EMI_state, health, uncertainty_force, uncertainty_torque, validity_domain}

Mechanism-specific internal variables remain private to the module.

No experimental propulsion module may contribute assumed force to a vehicle model outside its validated domain without an explicit HYPOTHESIS or SIMULATED classification.

## 7. Momentum and energy ledgers

Each propulsion or force-producing experiment SHALL maintain a momentum ledger:

F_measured = F_mass + F_EM + F_photon + F_boundary + F_environmental + F_artifact + F_residual

Each powered experiment SHALL maintain an energy ledger:

E_source = E_kinetic + E_thermal + E_EM + E_stored + E_exhaust + E_loss + E_residual

For established mechanisms, unexplained residuals are model/measurement discrepancies. For anomalous-force research, the residual itself is the experimental target and SHALL NOT be assigned to a new mechanism before conventional terms and artifacts are bounded.

## 8. ITX-01 interface families

The first canonical integrated test article is WS-ITX-01.

Every ITX-compatible module SHALL declare:

- I_P: power interface
- I_D: data interface
- I_M: mechanical interface
- I_C: control interface
- I_E: evidence/provenance interface

### I_P — Power

Record voltage, current, frequency where relevant, phase where relevant, power, accumulated energy, limits, switching transients, and isolation domain.

### I_D — Data

Every channel requires identifier, units, sample rate, clock source, uncertainty metadata, calibration reference, and raw-data retention location.

### I_M — Mechanical

Record mass, center of gravity, mount datum, coordinate transform, expected force axis, expected torque axis, and mounting configuration digest.

### I_C — Control

Hardware interlocks outrank deterministic local controllers; deterministic local control outranks optimization; AI/learning layers may recommend or optimize only inside declared bounds.

### I_E — Evidence

Each run links test article, material batch, geometry, firmware/software, solver version, calibration, sensor manifest, environment, commands, raw data, processed data, uncertainty model, controls, hypotheses, results, and claims.

## 9. Result classes

- WS-R0 APPARATUS_LIMITED: apparatus or calibration cannot resolve the required effect.
- WS-R1 CONVENTIONAL_OR_NULL: data support a null result or known conventional explanation.
- WS-R2 MODEL_CORRELATED: measurement and model agree within declared uncertainty and validity domain.
- WS-R3 UNEXPLAINED_DISCREPANCY: statistically/engineering-significant residual remains after modeled effects and controls.
- WS-R4 INDEPENDENTLY_REPRODUCED_DISCREPANCY: unexplained effect reproduced using an independent apparatus/operator or otherwise independent experimental path.

WS-R4 is not by itself proof of new physics; it authorizes deeper mechanism investigation.

## 10. Solver credibility ladder

- S0: dimensional and unit consistency.
- S1: analytical limiting cases pass.
- S2: numerical convergence demonstrated.
- S3: cross-solver/reference comparison acceptable.
- S4: calibrated against physical coupon/bench data.
- S5: blind prediction passes on data not used in calibration.
- S6: independent reproduction or external validation.

Model maturity SHALL be recorded separately from product/technology maturity.

## 11. Physical Engineering Loop Benchmark

Worldshepherd SHALL track:

- T_H: hypothesis to first executable model.
- T_P: hypothesis to first physical prototype.
- T_V: hypothesis to validated result.
- C_V: cost per validated result/design.
- E_P: prediction error against physical measurements.
- P_R: provenance completeness.
- R_R: reproducibility rate.
- D_R: design-space coverage per engineering hour.
- F_R: failure/null-data reuse rate.
- A_R: safely automated fraction of the workflow.

A Worldshepherd competitive claim of being "better" must eventually be stated in these or similarly measurable terms rather than by architectural breadth alone.

## 12. Materials/process/property graph

Each physical material record SHOULD support:

FEEDSTOCK -> PROCESS_HISTORY -> MICROSTRUCTURE -> PROPERTY_SET -> COMPONENT -> SYSTEM_PERFORMANCE

Properties may include mechanical, thermal, electrical, magnetic, RF/dielectric, chemical/environmental, manufacturing-quality, and application-specific compatibility measurements.

Predicted properties SHALL be distinguishable from measured properties.

## 13. FIELD-SKIN maturity split

- FS-1: environmental/field sensing.
- FS-2: validated adaptive reflection/transmission/null/communications behavior.
- FS-3: validated contribution to propulsion, flow, energy, or other higher-level physical control.

FS-1/FS-2 evidence SHALL NOT be used to imply FS-3 performance without physical validation.

## 14. Deterministic exploration / Glob

Worldshepherd may use deterministic numerical target graphs and permutation operators to generate experiment order, withheld sets, reproducibility seeds, and DOE trajectories.

Glob-derived numbers SHALL NOT become physical constants or mechanism parameters merely because they coincide with values in technical literature.

Any advantage over standard DOE methods (random, Latin hypercube, Sobol, Bayesian optimization, etc.) must be demonstrated experimentally or computationally using coverage, bias detection, convergence, cost, or predictive-performance metrics.

## 15. First integrated campaign

ITX01-CAMPAIGN-A SHALL aim to:

1. Establish calibrated force/metrology behavior.
2. Characterize powered-null and environmental artifacts.
3. Operate one validated-force propulsion article.
4. Capture synchronized power, thermal, magnetic, environmental, and force data.
5. Operate FIELD-SKIN sensing in parallel where available.
6. Compare predictions against measurements.
7. Execute deterministic DOE ordering and withheld conditions.
8. Inject bounded faults and validate safe response.
9. Preserve complete provenance in SARA.
10. Perform at least one blind prediction.

## 16. Promotion doctrine

Physics before optimization.
Measurement before extraordinary interpretation.
Materials are measured, not assumed.
AI corrects and extends physics models before it replaces them.
Every promoted result must be reproducible from provenance.
A failed hypothesis remains valuable if its configuration and evidence are preserved.
