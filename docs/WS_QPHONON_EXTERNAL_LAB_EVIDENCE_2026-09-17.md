## Harvard — single SiV acoustic Purcell experiment

Joe et al., *Nature* 653, 378–383 (2026), DOI 10.1038/s41586-026-10495-7.

Publisher-reported measurements include:
- a single SiV spin embedded in a diamond nanomechanical/optomechanical structure;
- approximately 12 GHz acoustic resonance;
- roughly tenfold faster spin relaxation on resonance;
- use of the SiV as a probe of the phonon spectrum up to 28 GHz.

Springer Nature provides source-data links for Figs. 2–4. The source-data binary files were **not locally acquired in this session**, so the local evidence state is publisher-verified measurement extraction rather than raw-data custody.

## Harvard — all-mechanical SiV coherence protection

Cornell et al., *Nature Physics* 22, 1493–1497 (2026), DOI 10.1038/s41567-026-03369-2.

Reported experimental values used in the ledger:
- bare-state T2* = 0.68 ± 0.04 microseconds;
- dressed-state T2* = 2.2 ± 0.2 microseconds;
- acoustic Rabi frequency reaching 800 MHz.

The central result is experimentally demonstrated all-mechanical coherence protection of a silicon-vacancy spin in diamond.

## Yale — ultracoherent GHz phonons

Luo et al., *Nature Physics* 22, 1486–1492 (2026), DOI 10.1038/s41567-026-03314-3.

Reported laboratory values include:
- phonon frequency near 12.66 GHz;
- maximum Q = 247 million;
- linewidth = 51.3 Hz;
- coherence time = 6.2 ms;
- energy decay time = 3.12 ms;
- negligible measured dephasing contribution;
- approximately tenfold reduction in surface loss after optimized polishing.

The extracted values pass an internal arithmetic consistency check:

- Q/(pi f) = 6.21 ms, matching the reported 6.2-ms coherence time;
- Q/(2 pi f) = 3.105 ms, matching the reported 3.12-ms energy decay time.
