2. `SIMULATED ONLY` -> physical capability status requires measured lab evidence; software output is insufficient.
3. Historical internal software reports do not supersede newer repository state.
4. Partner validation requires externally attributable evidence; outreach or brochures do not qualify.
5. Legal/IP conclusions remain subject to qualified legal review.
6. Commercial valuation remains a planning estimate until supported by market transactions, independent valuation, or partner/investor evidence.
