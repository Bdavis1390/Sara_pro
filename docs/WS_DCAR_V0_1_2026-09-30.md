## Mission

WS-DCAR is a vendor-neutral assurance layer for data-center resource and flexibility commitments. It does not replace schedulers, DCIM/BMS platforms, DERMS, demand-response aggregators, or utility controls. Its role is to preserve and test the evidence required to determine whether a claimed event actually satisfied an authorized commitment.

## Trust chain

```text
request
  -> PRIME authorization
  -> SARA orchestration
  -> facility / compute / storage / cooling actuation
  -> ECHO SENTINEL LINK evidence and provenance
  -> OVERWATCH verification / disposition
  -> signed audit package
```

## Measurement model

The first reference gate distinguishes:

- `delta_P_grid`: instantaneous reduction at the grid boundary;
- `delta_E_grid`: event energy effect;
- workload reduction or migration;
- battery substitution;
- on-site generation substitution;
- HVAC contribution;
- rebound energy after the event.

Two events that both report "20 MW curtailed" are not assumed equivalent when their mechanisms, baselines, configuration state, authorization, or rebound differ.

## Evidence minimum

A field candidate should preserve at least:

- source organization and dataset/project identity;
- request source and request ID;
- timestamps with clock-source provenance;
- human/machine authorization state and authorization evidence;
- policy/configuration hashes;
- hardware, firmware and software manifest identity;
- baseline method and uncertainty;
- authoritative meter identity/provenance at the declared measurement boundary;
- command and measured-response timestamps;
- source decomposition;
- event duration;
- rebound energy;
- telemetry gaps and stale-data markers;
- operator overrides;
- exception disposition;
- evidence hash/signature and audit reference.

## FLEX-001

`research/ws_dcar_flex_001/` provides a standard-library reference verifier, time-series replay path, representative synthetic trace, external-surrogate adapter, partner-evidence intake contract, and pytest regression suite.

The verifier can return only:

- `VERIFIED`
- `VERIFIED_WITH_EXCEPTIONS`
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`
- `NONCOMPLIANT`

The design intentionally refuses to collapse missing evidence into a pass/fail claim.

### Time-series assurance

The trace verifier additionally tests whether evidence remains coherent throughout an event. It detects:

- missing source, request or authorization evidence;
- configuration drift;
- meter-identity changes;
- stale telemetry and excessive gaps;
- late response or duration shortfall;
- unauthorized successful actuation;
- generator substitution;
- mismatch between integrated grid-boundary reduction and the energy attributed to individual mechanisms.

This prevents an apparently correct aggregate result from masking an untraceable or materially different execution path.

### Partner-origin evidence contract

`partner_bundle.py` binds an external event manifest to the replay evidence. A declared source/project, configuration identity and meter identity cannot override what the trace actually contains. The contract also records the measurement boundary and preserves claim scope: device/node/cluster/PDU/facility observations are not promoted into grid-boundary claims.

A known unauthorized action and missing authorization evidence remain distinct:

- known unauthorized action -> `NONCOMPLIANT`;
- authorization evidence absent/invalid -> `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.

The staged protocol is documented in `docs/WS_DCAR_NLR_ARGONNE_VALIDATION_PROTOCOL_2026-09-30.md`: NLR-001 first, followed by Argonne JLSE ANL-001.

## External validation gate

Before any facility/grid field-performance claim, validate with a partner dataset or sandbox that provides:

1. source/project identity and immutable source custody;
2. authoritative measurement at the claimed boundary;
3. known control commands and request provenance;
4. authorization evidence;
5. facility/workload mechanism telemetry where available;
6. synchronized timestamps and clock provenance;
7. configuration identity/custody;
8. documented baseline rules and uncertainty;
9. one or more injected or naturally occurring adverse cases.

A lower-boundary experiment can validate WS-DCAR behavior at that boundary, but cannot advance a higher-level facility/grid claim.
