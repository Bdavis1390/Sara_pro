# xTech|Search 10 Submission Checklist — WS-EDGE

**Deadline:** October 19, 2026, 5:00 PM ET
**Status:** Development active; submission not authorized until every hard gate below is verified.

## Eligibility gate

- [ ] For-profit U.S. business legally exists.
- [ ] Meets 13 C.F.R. § 121.702 small-business requirements.
- [ ] Ownership/control requirement verified.
- [ ] Company plus affiliates has no more than 500 employees.
- [ ] Prior/current/pending substantially-similar federal funding or proposal status reviewed.
- [ ] Any required disclosure to Army FUZE xTech Program Office completed.
- [ ] Only one xTech|Search 10 submission will be made for the entity.

## White-paper gate

- [ ] Official Valid Eval white-paper DOCX template obtained.
- [ ] Three-page limit confirmed in the rendered final document.
- [ ] Company name exactly matches desired marketing/legal presentation.
- [ ] Proposal title exactly matches desired marketing presentation.
- [ ] Introduction addresses problem and technology without unsupported claims.
- [ ] Army Benefits directly map to C2/Counter-C2 and Adaptive Sustainment.
- [ ] Technical Approach receives the largest share of page space.
- [ ] Commercial Potential identifies real dual-use path.
- [ ] Claims states and current evidence are internally checked.
- [ ] No CUI, export-controlled, partner-proprietary, or private material is included.

## Scoring red-team

Army weighting:

| Criterion | Weight | Internal question |
|---|---:|---|
| Introduction | 5% | Is the product and problem understandable immediately? |
| Army Benefits | 25% | Does the paper clearly improve an Army mission outcome? |
| Technical Approach | 40% | Is there a credible, measurable engineering path rather than architecture prose? |
| Commercial Potential | 25% | Is there a realistic non-Army market and product model? |
| Proposal Quality | 5% | Is it concise, internally consistent, and template-compliant? |

Internal release rule: no section may rely on implied certification, partner validation, fielding, CUI authority, or physical performance not supported by exact evidence.

## Submission evidence

Retain:

- final submitted file;
- SHA-256 digest;
- final source commit;
- human approval record;
- eligibility evidence references (private, not committed publicly);
- disclosure record if required;
- submission timestamp;
- confirmation/receipt;
- any Army evaluator feedback.

The public repository should store status and non-sensitive evidence references only, not protected corporate identifiers or portal credentials.
