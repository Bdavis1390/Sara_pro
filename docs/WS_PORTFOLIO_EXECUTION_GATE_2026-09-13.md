## Program 2 — Qualification Factory

Mission: convert concepts into falsifiable, reproducible evidence packages.

### Programmable electromagnetic surface
Current concept: reconfigurable tiles with controllable phase/amplitude/material state and closed-loop sensing. Qualification begins with simulation and benign laboratory measurements of response, bandwidth, loss, update latency, thermal drift, repeatability, and power. No performance claim advances without measured uncertainty and reproducibility.

### WS-AlTi programmable deposited alloy
Current commercial state remains IP-stage. Value gates are:
1. prior-art and IP review;
2. process-window modeling;
3. coupon design;
4. fabrication by a qualified partner;
5. composition/microstructure characterization;
6. mechanical/thermal/environmental testing;
7. repeatability;
8. partner review.

No safety-critical qualification claim is permitted before process- and grade-specific evidence exists.

### Autonomous platforms and robotics
Every subsystem must pass a requirements ledger covering mass, energy, thermal limits, structure, sensing, control, maintainability, manufacturability, supply chain, safety, and mission-specific performance. Biomimicry and novel architectures are search heuristics, not evidence of superiority.

### Speculative physics lanes
Mathematical decompositions and simulation constructs remain mathematical tools unless tied to conventional physics, finite-energy constraints, stable solutions, and reproducible measurement. Anomalous physical claims require artifact-controlled experiments and independent replication.
