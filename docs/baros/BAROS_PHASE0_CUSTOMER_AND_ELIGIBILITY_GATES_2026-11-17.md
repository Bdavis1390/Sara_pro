# BAROS EvidenceGate — Phase 0 market, applicant and STTR/SBIR intake gates

**Date:** 2026-11-17. **Public-safe, exploratory and unfunded.** No customer or clinician interview, paid pilot, formal company, grant application, letter of support, IP license or funding agreement is implied by this artifact. These are documented prerequisites in response to Dr. Ming Zhao's November 13 review.

## Business hypothesis and buyer (unvalidated)

**Product:** research-stage, version-locked evidence-qualification/abstention middleware before biological adaptive planning. **Not:** autonomous plan optimization, FDA-cleared medical software, replacement for clinical TPS/QA, a provider of treatment recommendations, or accredited clinical verification.

**Candidate end user:** radiation therapy medical-physics group / translational planning research team. **Possible budget owner:** cancer center radiation oncology research program, clinical physics department or TPS technology company. These are hypotheses, not established purchasers or willing customers.

**Problem statement to test:** longitudinal biological inputs may be rejected inconsistently or with inadequate traceability, and current gate mechanisms may not make biological model identifiability and source-dependency invalidation explicit. Existing tools may already address this adequately; no unmet market need is proven. Discovery must actively invite this negative conclusion.

## Five-interview minimum qualitative discovery plan (not completed)

At least five independent interviews drawn from: clinical medical physicist, head-and-neck radiation oncologist, treatment-planning workflow owner/dosimetrist, radiation oncology informatics or QMS/safety specialist, and a TPS vendor or R&D purchaser. Avoid counting one joint team call as five independent interviews. Each prospective participant decides whether to participate and may decline; no requests for PHI or confidential protocols.

**Neutral questions to ask (in a 15–25 minute email or remote exchange):**

1. What treatment-planning decisions, if any, use biological or longitudinal imaging evidence at your site, and how are data and model uncertainties documented?
2. Can you recall a generic failure type where source validity/identifiability or registration uncertainty changed a research planning decision? Please describe without patient identifiers.
3. Which safeguards or products already prevent these errors? Where are they reliable, burdensome, or insufficient?
4. What false-abstention rate or workflow burden would render a pre-TPS gate unusable in research?
5. If an independent team could measure one incremental benefit, which endpoint would be persuasive? What would disprove the need for this product?
6. Who decides whether a product of this kind can be trialed, bought or integrated, and what certifications/regulatory obligations would apply?
7. Would a bounded research-only evaluation be useful enough to justify spending staff time, and if so who would sponsor or fund it? A positive response is not a purchase commitment.

**Interview record fields:** date/time, participant role and institution with permission, consent for note use, current workflow and safeguards, failure category and frequency (only participant-provided), buyer/budget owner, independent validation endpoint, integration and IP objections, willingness to consider a pilot (nonbinding), contradictory findings and follow-up status.

**Exit gate for product claim:** demonstrate a reproducible, anonymized set of actual interview findings, a named buyer role and credible willingness to test/pay, documented competitors, and independently comparable workflow outcomes. Five interviews alone do not constitute a statistically representative market study or demonstrate commercial demand. Until then market status = **OPEN**.

## NIH workshare design: not a commitment or award

- **SBIR Phase I** typically requires **at least two-thirds of research work** to be performed by the SBC and PI primary employment with the small business; verify exact NOFO, budget, subcontracting rules and exceptions before application.
- **STTR** requires **at least 40% of work by eligible SBC** and **at least 30% by a qualified nonprofit research institution**, with specific IP/workshare agreements and eligible PI affiliation. A larger university-controlled TPS/dosimetry project may favor STTR *structurally* but cannot remove small-business eligibility.
- **Current parent opportunities:** NCI lists PA-27-100 (SBIR) and PA-27-102 (STTR) with a standard January 5, 2027 receipt date. These are not submissions or awards. Given an unformed applicant and uncommitted clinical partner, **January is a planning reference, not an authorized or credible filing promise**.
- Project budget must be developed from actual proposed tasks, institutional staffing/indirect rates, permitted software development costs, clinical-physics instrumentation/access, dataset licensing and commercial review; no invented total or guaranteed salary.
- No NCI submission until both the legal applicant and independently agreed technical workshare are documented and validated.
