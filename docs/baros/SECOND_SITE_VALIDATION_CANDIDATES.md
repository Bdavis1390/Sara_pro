Initial outreach should remain disciplined: allow the current Dr. Chenyang Shen / UT Southwestern partnership proposal to mature while maintaining a ready escalation list.

## Outreach sequencing

1. Allow Dr. Shen a reasonable response window to the active collaboration proposal.
2. Use NCI mechanism guidance to refine the collaboration structure.
3. If Dr. Shen declines, does not respond after a disciplined follow-up, or recommends broader expertise, contact Priority 1.
4. Bring Priority 2 in when multi-site validation, QA architecture, or independent automated-planning deployment expertise becomes necessary.
5. Consider Priority 3 for senior scientific review, referral, or biology-guided planning expertise.

## Non-negotiable outreach rule

Do not ask any candidate to endorse BAROS or certify a predetermined conclusion. Ask them to design, challenge, test, reproduce, and, where appropriate, partner on independently governed validation. Funding must be tied to actual scientific workshare, never to a favorable result.
