A successful outbound message establishes only **SUBMITTED / PUBLIC COMMENT SENT** when delivery evidence exists. It does **not** establish NIST receipt beyond available mail evidence, substantive review, acceptance, endorsement, standards participation, conformity, adoption, or validation.

Any NIST response must be retained as separate external evidence and interpreted according to its actual wording.

Do not submit if any release condition remains open, the source text cannot be verified, restricted information is present, the AI-assistance disclosure is removed or inaccurate, or CRE1AWS has not explicitly authorized the external action.
