# WS-QPHONON Quality and Reliability V0.2\n\n## Claim boundary\n\nThe phrase 10x better is treated as an engineering target. This profile defines ten independently testable quality and reliability dimensions; it does not claim a demonstrated tenfold improvement in physical quantum performance.\n\n## Ten quality dimensions\n\n1. Freeze a pre-run manifest before an experiment advances.\n2. Bind configuration, parameter snapshot, and acceptance criteria by deterministic digests.\n3. Use an explicit experiment lifecycle state machine.\n4. Prohibit skipping PRIME or human approval states.\n5. Make COMPLETED and ABORTED terminal outcomes.\n6. Use deterministic canonical serialization for manifest and event verification.\n7. Keep versioned evidence schemas so V0.1 remains identifiable while V0.2 is stricter.\n8. Preserve missing decision-critical parameters as missing rather than synthesizing them.\n9. Fall back to CHARACTERIZE when drift or model discrepancy leaves the validated envelope.\n10. Require regression plus adversarial CI before review promotion.\n\n## What improved\n\nThe L2 architecture already had claims control, registry normalization, PRIME screening, ECHO provenance fields, and passive drift classification. V0.2 adds pre-run immutability, explicit lifecycle transition enforcement, strict evidence integrity, separate execution approval, stronger numerical validation, supply-chain pinning, and deterministic adversarial coverage.\n\n## What is not claimed\n\nNo quality control in V0.2 improves SiV or SnV coherence by itself, raises phononic transfer fidelity by itself, proves partner hardware performance, or upgrades QPHONON to L3 physical capability.\n
