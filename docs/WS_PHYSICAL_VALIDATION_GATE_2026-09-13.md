# Worldshepherd Physical Research Validation Gate — 2026-09-13

Status: CANONICAL R&D EVIDENCE GATE

## Purpose
Apply one falsifiable qualification method across Worldshepherd physical-system research. This file prevents mathematical elegance, simulation output, concept imagery, software implementation, or market interest from being misrepresented as physical validation.

Canonical chain:
`requirement -> hypothesis -> test article -> instrumentation -> configuration -> result -> uncertainty -> pass/fail -> provenance -> independent/human review`

## Maturity labels
Use only the existing Worldshepherd claims states:
- PROVEN INTERNALLY
- IMPLEMENTED IN SOFTWARE
- SUPPORTED BY LITERATURE
- SIMULATED ONLY
- HYPOTHESIS
- SPECULATIVE EXTENSION
- REQUIRES LAB VALIDATION
- REQUIRES PARTNER VALIDATION
- REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW
- NOT CURRENTLY CLAIMED

Physical performance cannot be promoted from IMPLEMENTED IN SOFTWARE or SIMULATED ONLY without measured evidence.

## Lane A — Programmable electromagnetic surface

### Current technical basis
A tile is modeled as a reconfigurable electromagnetic element with complex amplitude/phase response. Coupling between tiles can be represented through mutual electromagnetic interaction, shared feed/control structures, and coordinated control. The engineering interpretation is a programmable electromagnetic boundary condition rather than a claim of exotic physics.

### Benign qualification sequence
1. Electromagnetic simulation of one cell and a small array.
2. Fabricate a passive or tunable laboratory article.
3. Calibrate instrumentation and reference standards.
4. Measure S-parameters and commanded phase/amplitude states.
5. Characterize bandwidth, insertion/reflection loss, tuning range, update latency, thermal drift, repeatability, and power.
6. Compare measured fields against predicted constructive/destructive patterns.
7. Record uncertainty, failure cases, and configuration digest.
8. Repeat on a separately assembled article before promotion.

### Pass condition
A commanded field response must be reproduced within a predeclared tolerance across repeated runs and at least two independently assembled configurations.

### Claims boundary
Do not equate laboratory field shaping with broadband invisibility, operational stealth, a cloak, force production, or any other unmeasured system-level effect.

## Lane B — Prime-indexed / modal mathematics

### Current status
Mathematical decomposition only unless and until a physical observable is defined and experimentally distinguished from conventional modal analysis.

### Required physical admissibility
- finite total energy;
- bounded coupling;
- stable thermal/material response;
- convergent state evolution;
- dimensionally consistent observables;
- conservation-law accounting.

### Pass condition
The formalism must either produce a quantitatively better prediction than a conventional baseline on held-out data or remain labeled as a mathematical representation with no independent physical claim.

## Lane C — WS-AlTi programmable deposited alloy

### Current commercial/technical status
IP-stage concept requiring modeling, coupon fabrication, and partner/laboratory validation before material-property or valuation promotion.

### Qualification sequence
1. Prior-art and freedom-to-operate screen.
2. Define composition/process windows and manufacturability constraints.
3. Generate process/material predictions with uncertainty bounds.
4. Produce a coupon matrix using a qualified deposition partner.
5. Record full process provenance: feedstock lot, machine, atmosphere, thermal history, scan/deposition parameters, post-processing, and specimen orientation.
6. Characterize composition, porosity, phase/microstructure, grain morphology, and defects.
7. Measure mechanical, thermal, environmental, and fatigue properties appropriate to intended use.
8. Replicate the best-performing window across independent build lots.
9. Compare against explicit conventional-alloy baselines.

### Pass condition
No property claim advances beyond REQUIRES LAB VALIDATION until repeatability and baseline superiority are demonstrated on a defined metric with uncertainty.

## Lane D — Drones, humanoids, companion and autonomous platforms

### Universal subsystem ledger
Every design must close, at minimum:
- mission requirements;
- mass budget;
- center-of-mass/inertia budget;
- energy and peak-power budget;
- thermal budget;
- structural loads and factors of safety;
- actuator authority;
- sensing and observability;
- control stability;
- communications and degraded-mode behavior;
- maintainability;
- manufacturability;
- supply-chain alternatives;
- software safety boundaries;
- human interaction hazards;
- environmental limits.

### Design rule
When a subsystem fails, redesign the failed subsystem or the system architecture; do not conceal the deficit by relaxing the baseline after the fact.

### Biomimicry rule
Biological analogues may generate design hypotheses for morphology, sensing, locomotion, energy use, redundancy, or swarm coordination. They do not establish performance until engineering tests do.

### Pass condition
No '10x' or industry-leading assertion without a named baseline, metric, test method, and measured result.

## Lane E — Propulsion, plasma, electromagnetic and anomalous-force research

### Conventional lanes
Model and test conventional electromagnetic, electrostatic, plasma, fluid, and propulsive mechanisms using accepted conservation laws and calibrated energy/momentum accounting.

### Anomalous-force gate
Any closed-system force claim requires artifact controls before promotion:
- calibrated force measurement;
- thermal-drift characterization;
- cable/current-force controls;
- magnetic/environmental controls;
- reversed or mirrored configurations;
- powered dummy/control article;
- blind or randomized runs where practical;
- independent replication.

### Pass condition
A residual signal must exceed the full uncertainty/artifact budget and replicate independently before it can be described as anomalous. Until then it remains HYPOTHESIS or INCONCLUSIVE.

## Lane F — Quantum, advanced materials, sensing and energy concepts

### Rule
Separate peer-reviewed/laboratory support for a mechanism from Worldshepherd-specific implementation evidence.

### Required progression
literature support -> model -> bench article -> calibrated measurement -> reproducibility -> partner/independent validation -> application-specific qualification.

## Portfolio-wide stop conditions
A claim must not be promoted when any of the following applies:
- missing raw data or configuration lineage;
- post-hoc metric selection without disclosure;
- uncalibrated instrumentation;
- unresolved conservation-law discrepancy;
- only one successful run;
- only simulation evidence for a physical claim;
- missing negative/failure evidence;
- dependence on an undisclosed assumption that materially changes the result.

## Evidence package minimum
Every physical qualification package should contain:
1. requirement and claim being tested;
2. prior maturity label;
3. hypothesis and competing explanations;
4. test article bill of materials/configuration;
5. instrumentation and calibration record;
6. procedure;
7. raw and processed data references;
8. uncertainty/error analysis;
9. pass/fail thresholds declared before interpretation;
10. negative/anomalous observations;
11. software/model commit IDs;
12. operator/reviewer identities;
13. final maturity decision and rationale.
