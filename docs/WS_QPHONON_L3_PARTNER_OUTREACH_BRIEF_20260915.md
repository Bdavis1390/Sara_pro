# WS-QPHONON L3 Partner Outreach Brief — 2026-09-15

## Claims boundary

Worldshepherd is seeking a **governed experimental collaboration**, not claiming ownership of a quantum-phononic hardware platform or asking a partner to endorse an unvalidated Worldshepherd physical capability.

Current state: `ARCHITECTURE_INTEGRATED_PHYSICAL_CAPABILITY_NOT_CLAIMED`.

The proposed contribution is the SARA / PRIME / ECHO / OVERWATCH experiment-governance layer:

- experiment orchestration and pre-run configuration freeze;
- uncertainty-aware authorization gates;
- parameter/provenance custody;
- model-vs-measurement discrepancy tracking;
- holdout-prediction discipline;
- negative-result retention;
- human approval at physical-action boundaries.

## Primary target — Harvard / Lončar Lab

**Why this is the first target:** the lab is directly aligned with diamond color centers, quantum nanophotonics, optomechanics, and the SiV / lithium-niobate-on-diamond work underlying the present QPHONON hypothesis.

Public contact:
- Marko Lončar — `loncar@g.harvard.edu`
- Harvard SEAS profile: https://seas.harvard.edu/person/marko-loncar
- Laboratory for Nanoscale Optics: https://nano-optics.seas.harvard.edu/

### Proposed first ask

Request a short technical discussion to determine whether a **software-governed characterization pilot** could be useful around an existing or planned SiV/TFLN/phononic device.

The pilot should begin with characterization and holdout prediction, not with a claim of coherent two-node transfer.

Candidate scope:

1. partner identifies one existing measurement class suitable for controlled repetition;
2. both sides freeze the device state, parameter definitions, measurement protocol, and claims boundary;
3. Worldshepherd packages the experiment through SARA / PRIME / ECHO;
4. partner executes under its normal laboratory safety and authorization rules;
5. one subset of data calibrates the model;
6. a separately reserved holdout condition tests prediction;
7. discrepancies are retained and reported without post-hoc threshold changes.

### Minimum useful outcome

A governed, reproducible characterization result with full provenance and a precommitted holdout test. This would advance the **governance/runtime evidence** and could advance the **phononic physics axis** only if the actual device mechanism is relevant to QPHONON.

## Partner-facing technical hypothesis

> Continuous acoustic protection and tunable phononic coupling in group-IV diamond spin systems may support a dynamically switchable protected-memory / transfer interface. Worldshepherd's proposed role is not to replace the qubit or resonator specialists, but to provide a bounded experimental-control and evidence layer that measures device state, checks uncertainty and operating limits, freezes pre-run conditions, records provenance, compares prediction against holdout data, and prevents software success from being mislabeled as physical capability.

## What we should not ask for initially

Do not lead with:

- a claim that Worldshepherd has a quantum computer;
- a request to validate a 99% transfer number;
- a demand for device access before technical fit is established;
- a request to transfer partner IP;
- a claim that SARA improves coherence by itself;
- a statement that generic QPU results prove phononic physics;
- an attempt to bypass the partner's laboratory safety or experiment-approval process.

## Evidence gate for any collaboration

No partner reply, meeting, NDA, proposal, award, device reservation, simulator run, or generic-QPU run promotes QPHONON to L3.

L3 requires relevant physical hardware, Worldshepherd governance participation, a reproducible result, successful precommitted holdout prediction, uncertainty bounds, full ECHO provenance, and independent partner/human reproduction.
