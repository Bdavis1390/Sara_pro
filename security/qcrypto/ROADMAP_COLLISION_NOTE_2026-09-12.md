# QCRYPTO Roadmap Collision Note — 2026-09-12

## Finding

IonQ's public roadmap targets approximately 20,000 physical qubits and 1,600 logical qubits in 2028. IonQ's separate Walking Cat secp256k1 resource estimate reports approximately 19,397 physical qubits, about 1,450 logical qubits, and 25.7 days per modeled attempt under that architecture.

## Claims boundary

This is a same-vendor roadmap-to-resource-envelope collision for migration planning. It is not demonstrated cryptanalytic capability, not Q-day, not a production-key break, and not proof that the 2028 roadmap target will be achieved or will satisfy every runtime and fault-tolerance assumption in the resource estimate.

## Control change

QCRYPTO now tracks roadmap maturity separately from demonstrated hardware and can raise migration urgency when a roadmap numerically reaches a published attack envelope. Roadmap evidence alone cannot promote Q2, Q3, or Q4.

## Required action

Use this collision as a schedule-risk trigger: accelerate crypto inventory, PQ interoperability, critical-key migration planning, recovery drills, and independent verification of roadmap and attack assumptions while preserving claims control.
