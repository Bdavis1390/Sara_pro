## Required finding format

Each finding should include:

- reviewer or source;
- date;
- affected section;
- finding summary;
- severity: observation / minor / major / critical;
- reproducibility or evidence;
- proposed resolution;
- disposition: open / accepted / rejected with rationale / resolved;
- claims impact.
